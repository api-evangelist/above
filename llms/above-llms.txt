# Above Security

> Above is an AI-native insider risk platform that detects and investigates risky human and AI-agent activity across SaaS, identity, and endpoints — turning weeks of alert correlation into investigation-ready narratives, without invasive content capture.

Above focuses on intent-based insider risk: understanding what a person or AI agent was actually trying to do, not just flagging statistical anomalies. It is endpoint- and SaaS-native, privacy-aware (it captures behavioral signal rather than screen/keystroke content), and built for teams drowning in DLP and UEBA noise. Above is the inaugural sponsor of the Forscie Insider Threat Matrix and publishes the Insider Risk Index (insiderisk.io).

## Product
- [Real-time guidance](https://www.above.security/platform/real-time-guidance): In-the-moment user coaching that prevents risky actions before they happen.
- [AI investigative agents](https://www.above.security/investigative-agents): AI agents that assemble investigation-ready narratives from behavioral signal.

## Use cases
- [Insider risk use cases](https://www.above.security/use-case): Overview of the scenarios Above covers.
- [Malicious insider](https://www.above.security/malicious-insider): Detecting and investigating intentional insider data theft and sabotage.
- [Pre-departure / flight-risk data theft](https://www.above.security/pre-departure): Catching data exfiltration before a resignation.
- [Credential leaks](https://www.above.security/credential-leaks): Detecting leaked and misused credentials.
- [Shadow and personal AI use](https://www.above.security/personal-ai): Visibility into employees pasting sensitive data into personal AI tools.
- [Custom GPTs](https://www.above.security/custom-gpt): Risk from custom GPTs and unsanctioned AI assistants.
- [Agentic AI risk](https://www.above.security/agentic-ai): Monitoring autonomous AI agents that hold credentials and act on a user's behalf.
